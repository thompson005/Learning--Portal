WHAT IS CYBERSECURITY

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems, and data
from cyber threats, unauthorized access, and attacks. It ensures the confidentiality,
integrity, and availability (CIA) of digital assets.

What is Cybersecurity

Offensive security is an approach that involves simulating attacks to identify
vulnerabilities and weaknesses in a system, network or application before malicious
attackers can exploit them. Let's take the vending machine again, an offensive security
