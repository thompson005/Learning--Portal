Security in Software Development Lifecycle

Security in the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) refers to incorporating security practices and measures into every phase of the development process.

By embedding security early, teams can identify vulnerabilities and risks before they become costly issues, ensuring that the final product is secure from threats.
