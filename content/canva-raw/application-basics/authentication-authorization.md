WHAT IS AUTHORIZATION?

Authorization determines what resources or actions an
authenticated user or system has permission to access
or perform. It governs the privileges given to a user
after they have been authenticated.

you know which areas you’re allowed to go into. Are you
just a guest, or do you have backstage access? In the
digital world, this means what actions you can perform
once you’re logged in.

Single-Factor Authentication (SFA):

Using just one way to prove who you are. Usually, it’s just a username and password.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):

You prove who you are with two or more things. For example, after entering your
password, you also need to confirm with your phone (like receiving a code).

Biometric Authentication:

Instead of a password, you use something about you that can’t be copied, like your
fingerprint or face.

OAuth Authentication:

You use another app to prove who you are, like logging into a site using your Google or
Facebook account instead of making a new password.
