LINUX OVERVIEW

Linux Overview

Linux is a family of open-source operating systems based on the linux kernel(a kernel released by linus torvalds).It was first released on September 17,1991 and is the most common choice for servers and workstations.

The original linux kernel was designed by Linus because of a lack of a working kernel for GNU (a unix compatible OS made entirely of free software).
