Introduction

In the digital world, not every system is what it seems. Some systems are designed not to serve users — but to catch attackers. These are called honeypots.

A honeypot is a fake computer system or network setup to look like a real one. It tricks cybercriminals into thinking they’ve found a weak spot, but in reality, it’s a trap built to study their behavior, detect threats, and protect real systems.
