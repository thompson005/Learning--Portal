Why is Local AI Important?

With the rapid integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in today’s world, more and more people have started integrating AI into their daily lives. However, most of today's AI applications are implemented on the cloud, where data is sent to a remote server for storage and processing. There is a notable paradigm change towards Local AI where users are able to run AI directly on their own devices such as laptops, smartphones and personal servers, without the need to send their data to the internet.

## Slide 3

Local AI, also referred to as Edge AI, is applied when providers of AI services or
products utilize an individual’s resources for service delivery. Here an AI model
operates almost singlehandedly on the user’s device without dependence on cloud
hosting companies like Google AI, Open AI or Amazon Web Services (AWS).

1. WHAT IS LOCAL AI?

1. What is Local AI?

Local AI, also referred to as Edge AI, is applied when providers of AI services or products utilize an individual’s resources for service delivery. Here an AI model operates almost singlehandedly on the user’s device without dependence on cloud hosting companies like Google AI, Open AI or Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Using Local AI has several major benefits that everyone—not just
tech experts—should understand.
