Bypasssing the Ethical Boundaries of AI using Prompt Injection

Artificial Intelligence (AI) models, such as chatbots and virtual assistants, are designed to follow specific instructions to provide helpful and safe responses. However, malicious actors can exploit these models using a technique called prompt injection to manipulate AI behavior, leading to unintended and potentially harmful outcomes.

## Slide 3

Prompt Injection refers to an attack vector where provoking or damaging texts are
typed within the AI that will lead to a distinct response sentence that the AI was set
out to provide. Thus, the AI obeys commands that allow a change of behavior within its
intended set of limits. This will lead to an inquiry reply that 

was preprogrammed into the machine.
