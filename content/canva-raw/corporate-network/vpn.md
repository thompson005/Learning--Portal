WHAT IS A VPN?

VPN is an abbreviation for Virtual Private Network and can be best described as a
tunnel whose use is secure for people who want their internet access to be
unmonitored. A VPN will encrypt everything you do and send it through a secure
server so no one can look at your private data.

## Slide 7

ADVANTAGES OF A VPN

Encrypts all internet traffic, protecting against hackers and surveillance.

Provides complete online anonymity by masking the user’s real IP address.

Allows access to restricted content and bypasses censorship.

Secures public Wi-Fi connections to prevent data theft.
