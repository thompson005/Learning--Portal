## Slide 5

HOW VLAN WORKS

Switch ports are assigned to VLAN IDs 

(e.g., VLAN 10 for HR, VLAN 20 for IT).

VLAN tagging (using IEEE 802.1Q standard) allows VLAN traffic to
travel across trunk links between switches.

A Layer 3 device (router or Layer 3 switch) is needed to route traffic
between VLANs (inter-VLAN routing).

Each VLAN behaves like a separate LAN.
