IN SIMPLE WORDS

Maintaining access ensures attackers can stay in the system even after cleanup
efforts. They achieve this by automating their re-entry using scheduled tasks,
registry edits, or malicious scripts. Attackers also create hidden user accounts or
backdoors to regain control later. Advanced methods like rootkits 

help attackers stay hidden while maintaining their access.

In simple words

Maintaining access ensures attackers can stay in the system even after cleanup efforts. They achieve this by automating their re-entry using scheduled tasks, registry edits, or malicious scripts. help attackers stay hidden while maintaining their access.

EXAMPLE

On a personal computer, a hacker modifies the registry to launch their backdoor
program whenever the system starts, ensuring continued access after reboots.

Imagine a burglar breaks into a house and plants multiple spare keys under different
doormats or installs a secret door. Even if the homeowner changes the main lock
(cleans the system), the burglar can come back through these hidden entrances
(backdoors, scheduled tasks).
