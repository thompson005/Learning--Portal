FOOTPRINTING

Footprinting is the process of systematically
collecting information about a target’s systems,
networks, and environment to understand its
attack surface.

Footprinting is about collecting as much
information as possible about a target.

It helps attackers understand how a system is set
up and where the weak points are.

Think of it as creating a “blueprint” of a house before trying to break in.

RECONNAISSANCE

Reconnaissance refers to the broader activity
of collecting information about a target, often
focusing on specific details to prepare for
further actions.

May be a subset of footprinting
or used interchangeably in some
contexts.

Emphasizes targeted information
gathering, such as probing
specific systems, vulnerabilities,
or assets.

Can be passive (no interaction
with the target) or active (direct
interaction with the target).

KEY CHARACTERISTICS

Reconnaissance

Key Characteristics

Reconnaissance refers to the broader activity of collecting information about a target, often focusing on specific details to prepare for further actions.

May be a subset of footprinting or used interchangeably in some contexts.

Emphasizes targeted information gathering, such as probing specific systems, vulnerabilities, or assets.

Can be passive (no interaction with the target) or active (direct interaction with the target).
