Session Layer

The Session Layer (Layer 5) of the OSI Model is responsible for establishing, managing, and terminating communication sessions between two devices or applications over a network.

A session is essentially a connection for ongoing data exchange, and this layer ensures that the session stays open until all data is transmitted or until the session is manually closed.

The Session Layer acts as the “conversation manager” between two devices. It sets
up a connection, keeps it active while they’re communicating, and ends it when the

IN SIMPLE TERMS

In Simple Terms

The Session Layer acts as the “conversation manager” between two devices. It sets up a connection, keeps it active while they’re communicating, and ends it when the conversation is over.

For example, if you’re having a video call, the Session Layer is responsible for setting up and maintaining the connection until you end the call.
