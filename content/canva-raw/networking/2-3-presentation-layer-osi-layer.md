PRESENTATION LAYER

The Presentation Layer (Layer 6) of the OSI Model ensures that the data exchanged between systems is readable and in a proper format for the receiving system.

It focuses on the syntax and semantics of the information transmitted and is responsible for translating data formats, encrypting and decrypting data, and compressing or decompressing data for efficient transmission.
