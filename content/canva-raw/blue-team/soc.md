Security Operations Center (SOC)

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a team of cybersecurity professionals responsible for continuously monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cybersecurity threats and incidents within an organization. Their main goal is to protect the organization's assets, networks, and data from cyber threats.
