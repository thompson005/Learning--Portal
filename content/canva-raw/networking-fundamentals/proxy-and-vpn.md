In today’s digital world, online privacy and security are crucial. Two common tools used to protect internet users are Proxies and Virtual Private Networks (VPNs).

While both help in masking a user’s IP address and improving privacy, they function differently and serve distinct purposes.

WHAT IS A PROXY?

A proxy server is like a middleman between you and the internet. When you visit a
website using a proxy, your request goes through the proxy first before reaching the
website. That means the website only sees the proxy’s IP address, not yours.

## Slide 10

WHAT IS A VPN?

VPN is an abbreviation for Virtual Private Network and can be best described as a
tunnel whose use is secure for people who want their internet access to be
unmonitored. A VPN will encrypt everything you do and send it through a secure
server so no one can look at your private data.
