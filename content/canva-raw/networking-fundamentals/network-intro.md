## Slide 3

IN SIMPLE TERMS

A network is just a way for devices—like computers, phones, or printers—to talk to
each other. Think of a network like a group chat. Just like people send messages to
each other in the chat, computers in a network send information to one another.

In Simple Terms

A network is just a way for devices—like computers, phones, or printers—to talk to each other. Think of a network like a group chat. Just like people send messages to each other in the chat, computers in a network send information to one another.
