## Slide 4

TCP breaks down data into packets, adds headers for addressing and
sequencing, and then passes them to IP.

When the packets reach their destination, TCP reassembles them into the
original data stream.

IP encapsulates the packets with its own headers, including source
and destination IP addresses, and routes them across the network.

How it works

TCP breaks down data into packets, adds headers for addressing and sequencing, and then passes them to IP.
